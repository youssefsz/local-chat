How Local Chat handles your data

Before you open it.

Do we need the same Wi-Fi?

You need the same local network. Wi-Fi and Ethernet can work together. Guest networks, VPNs, and router isolation can prevent computers from finding each other.

Can I use it with someone far away?

Local Chat is built for nearby computers. It does not have a cloud relay or an internet messaging service.

Are the rooms private?

No. Everyone running Local Chat on the current network can see the rooms and their messages. Connections are encrypted, but nearby discovery does not verify a person’s real identity.

What happens when I close the window?

The app keeps receiving in the background. Choose Quit from the app menu to stop it. Your saved conversations remain on the computer.
