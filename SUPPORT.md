# Get help with Local Chat

Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/youssefsz/local-chat/issues/new?template=bug.yml) for reproducible problems.
For usage questions, open an [issue](https://github.com/youssefsz/local-chat/issues/new) and describe what you need help with. Maintainers apply the `question` label.
For vulnerabilities, use [private reporting](SECURITY.md).

## Check a connection

- Open Local Chat on both computers on the same Wi-Fi or Ethernet LAN.
- Allow Local Network access on macOS and incoming connections where required by your firewall.
- Choose **Reconnect** below the sidebar status or inside the People nearby panel.
- Check whether a guest network, client isolation, or VPN prevents local discovery.
- Read [platform status](docs/platform-status.md) for checks that remain pending.

Do not disable your firewall or share chat databases to obtain support.
Reports are public. Remove private messages, addresses, credentials, and device identifiers from attachments.

## Check a version

Settings shows the installed version and **Check for Updates**. Beta includes preview releases and newer stable versions.
Users of beta.5 and earlier need one manual upgrade to an updater-enabled version.
Linux Debian installations update through downloaded packages. AppImage updates require a writable installation.

Maintainers provide support as time permits. There is no paid support or guaranteed response time.
