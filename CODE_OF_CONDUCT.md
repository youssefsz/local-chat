# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect across issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.
Discuss the work and explain disagreements with evidence. Welcome people regardless of experience or background.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal attacks, and publication of another person's
private information are unacceptable. Do not pressure someone to share private chats or credentials to get help.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads, limit participation, or block accounts.
They consider the behavior, its impact, and repeated violations when deciding a response.

For ordinary moderation concerns, notify [@youssefsz](https://github.com/youssefsz) in the relevant thread.
For sensitive concerns, request a private contact from the maintainer without posting the details publicly.
Security vulnerabilities have a separate [private reporting process](SECURITY.md).
